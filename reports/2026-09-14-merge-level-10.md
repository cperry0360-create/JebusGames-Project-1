# Merging level 10 to main, and the two documents it made false

The ask was one line: `git checkout main && git merge --ff-only
claude/level-10-assets-2kqch4 && git push origin main`. It fast-forwarded, **all five
CI jobs are green on `main`, and the deploy ran rather than skipping** — so level 10
is live rather than merely merged, and the road shows ten built rows with no COMING
SOON for the first time.

The work beyond the command was two documents. **Neither `CLAUDE.md` nor
`claude/context.md` was touched by any of the nine commits**, so the moment the merge
landed both described a repository that no longer existed — `CLAUDE.md` in the
paragraph it added on 2026-09-13 specifically to stop that happening.

## The merge

`git merge-base origin/main origin/claude/level-10-assets-2kqch4` returned `d9611e4`,
which **was** `origin/main`'s head — 9 ahead, 0 behind, so `--ff-only` was the right
verb rather than a hopeful one.

One thing was checked before the push and is worth recording because it looked alarming
for a moment: **the local `main` was 31 commits stale**, so the fast-forward printed
`692a1fc..6100c78` rather than `d9611e4..6100c78` and the diffstat showed 144 files,
including all of level 9. That is the local clone catching up, not the merge dragging
in extra work. `git rev-list --count origin/main..main` returned **9** and the reverse
returned **0** before anything was pushed, and `main` and the branch head were verified
to be the same SHA.

All eight CI runs on the branch were green, including run 374 on the head.

### What landed

**Level 10, "AI Override: Part 2"** — 12 build pads, `level10.json`,
`map_level10.json`, `waves.level10.json`, a 3,440 px single lane, and Vlaude at
**36,000 hp** soaking **192/480 = 40.0%**. Also `src/systems/Vlaude.ts` (the rules
engine), `tests/level10.test.ts` (23 executed tests, not text-matched ones), 30
converted art files, and the level 9 assets and reports that were still only on the
branch.

**The number is not the same kind of number as the other nine, and the branch says so
in three places** (`enemies.json`'s `_health` note, the report, the commit message).
It was measured with Vlaude **walking the lane from the gate with no manipulation
firing**, because `systems/Vlaude.ts` is the rules and the scene does not play them.
It is a survivability check on the phase 3 walk. It is not comparable to levels 1–9
and it needs re-deriving when the fight lands.

### The sweep hazard did not fire, and it could not have

The standing fact says to check every open branch's `art.json` before deleting an
unreferenced asset. It did not apply: the merge-base was `origin/main`'s own head, so
the branch already contained every sweep `main` had done, and there was nothing to
restore. `tests/assets.test.ts` — the net that names missing keys — is green on `main`
after the merge.

**The hazard is now discharged for every level**, level 9 and level 10 both, and this
is the first time that has been true since it was written. It does not retire; it
applies to the next upload.

### The asset report's one open prediction, closed

`reports/2026-09-14-level-10-assets.md` could not weigh the deploy artifact from a
branch and left the check as one API call: run 366 on `main` uploaded 50,699,794 bytes,
the thirty new files are 7,641,900, **so the next `main` artifact should be about

It is **58,397,507**. The prediction was 58,341,694. **Off by 55,813 bytes — 0.1%** —
and the excess is the level 9 and level 10 data JSON and code, which the prediction did
not count because it was only about the art. The estimate was sound and the method is
worth reusing.

### What was NOT checked

**Nothing visual was re-measured for the level itself.** No `screens` run was made
here. Every frame-level claim about level 10 — that the board builds and draws, that
the world map holds ten levels with no road ahead, that nothing regressed at three
sizes in both orientations — rests on the harness runs recorded in the branch's own
report, not on anything re-measured in this session. This session merged, deployed,
and reconciled two documents; it did not re-audit.

**The live site was not fetched.** The Claude Code sandbox gets 403 from the egress
proxy to github.io, so the final 200 has to come from chat. What is proved here is
that the deploy job ran, that Pages reported success, and that the artifact is the
expected size.

Neither was touched by the nine commits — confirmed with
`git log --name-only d9611e4..6100c78 -- claude/context.md CLAUDE.md`, which returned
nothing. So both were true of `main` before the merge and false after it.

### `ui` and `buildall` are green, and this was measured rather than copied

`context.md` carried a table saying both scenarios exit 5 and are red, with diagnoses
(`A TAP ON THE HERO DOES NOT SELECT HIM`; `only 6 of 7 pads could be built on`, pad 3
at world 578,612 reporting `ringOnTap=false`). `reports/2026-09-14-level-10-assets.md`,
landing in the same merge, says both exit 0.

Two documents on `main` disagreeing is not something to resolve by preferring the newer
one, so both were re-run at 844x390 on `main` at `6100c78`:

| scenario | exit | RESULT | assertFails |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ui` | **0** | `the pad beats the ground, and the hero moves only when he has been picked up` | 0 |
| `buildall` | **0** | `every one of the 7 pads took a tower at 844x390` | 0 |
| `typegame` | **0** | `both panels raised, dismissed and reported` | 0 |

Neither old failure reproduces, and `buildall` explicitly reports 7 of 7 where it used
to report 6. The report is right and `context.md` was stale. **This closes the open
item `reports/2026-09-14-merge-level-9.md` carried as "red and undiagnosed"** — they
were fixed, not diagnosed, and by work already on `main`.

**One caveat is recorded with the green.** Both report `assertionsExpected: false`, so
neither is in `USES_EXPECT` and nothing forces them to have reached a check. Their
green rests on the `RESULT` line and the `assertFails` counter — weaker than
`typegame`'s guarantee, stronger than a bare exit 0. Promoting them was not asked for
and is not done.

## Where this leaves the repository

**DONE:** the merge, the deploy, both document reconciliations, the asset report's
artifact-size prediction, and the level-9 merge report's `ui`/`buildall` open item.

**IN FLIGHT:** nothing. Working tree clean, `main` green and deployed.

**OPEN — the big one:**

1. **The Vlaude fight is rules without a scene.** Eight pieces, in build order, in
   `reports/2026-09-14-level-10.md`. Everything `systems/Vlaude.ts` decides is tested
   and nothing plays it. Level 10's 40% must be re-derived afterwards, and **a re-run
   will not be enough**: the soak runner cannot express build lock (no lock state,
   only `occupied`), generate wall (its hero is a fixed point at `totalLength * 0.5`
   and never moves) or generate weapon (no tower health).

**OPEN — carried forward, none addressed here:**

2. **Level 8's east mouth is 88% of the lives lost**, and its losses are front-loaded —
   64% in waves 4 and 5, same cause. A wave-table pass, not a geometry one.
3. **The CEO's drones skip the review beam**, 66% of them, being summoned past it.
   Whether a summoned child inherits its parent's review is a design question nobody
   has been asked.
4. **`officeDrone` is the only enemy whose reached/reviewed counts disagree.**
5. **Pad counts now spread 3.1x** — 7 at level 1, 22 at level 7, **12 at level 10**,
   which is a new low for a late level and widens the spread the level-9 report
   flagged at 3x. Boss health is measured per board, so this is consistent rather than
   wrong, but the 14–15 habit is now clearly not a convention and is worth either
   adopting or abandoning out loud.
6. **Twenty canvas-vs-ink content boxes**, worst `icon-firerate` at 34.8%. None of the
   twenty is level 10's — `tools/measure_art.py` checks all 186 box-carrying entries
   and level 10 is clean.
7. **Cake tiers are probably too generous**, and the verdict line and the 2-cake tier
   disagree at exactly half: a run finishing 10 of 20 gets two cakes under the words
   "Barely standing."

**OPEN — repository hygiene:**

8. **Five unmerged remote branches remain**, and `claude/level-10-assets-2kqch4` joins
   `level-8-soft-lock-9bmho0` and `level-9-geometry-uac8ax` as fully contained in

   `deployment-status-review-a661d6` **326 ahead** (context said 7), uninspected since
